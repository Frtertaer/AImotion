# ANC — director's brief

Source video: a Twitch clip — streamer Bratishkin (dark room, purple LED,
Labubu charm on the mic) watching an Apple AirPods keynote (white void,
"5 hours / Active Noise Cancellation" giant type), wrapped in RU chat,
donations (10 555 ₽, 5 000 ₽), WINLINE banners.

Meaning: two broadcast worlds stacked in one frame — a sterile white
corporate keynote selling *silence* and a messy human stream full of noise.
The subject is literally what gets into the ear (he touches his ear).

Film: Active Noise Cancellation as a motion principle.
- A  Noise field  (0–8 s): stream of RU chat glyphs, donations, emotes,
  banners — visual noise over the room panel (stacked source: hooded
  streamer top / white keynote card bottom).
- B  Carrier     (8–14 s): a white keynote pulse emits the inverse wave;
  glyphs collapse at the front, noise wave flattens to a zero line.
- C  White field (14–21.8 s): silence wins. Paper field, "5 HOURS" +
  "ACTIVE NOISE CANCELLATION", AirPod bud, hairline zero line.
- D  Return      (21.8–29 s): noise floods back; NOISE CANCELLED /
  NOISE RETURNED; last glyph dies at the loop point.
